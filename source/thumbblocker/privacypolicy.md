title: Thumb Blocker Launcher Privacy Policy
date: 2024-04-04 17:04:56
---

Thumb Blocker Launcher does no tracking, telemetry or other monitoring of user activity, other than to process Thumb Blocker Pro upgrades.

No information about the user is sent to Seed Gang Games during day-to-day usage of the app - only during purchases, or when checking if a purchase had been made previously.

When interacting with the Google Play Billing services (which is used to process in-app purchases, such as upgrading to Thumb Blocker Pro), data is sent to Google and processed under accordance to their privacy policy.

Google have various privacy policies regarding billing and processing, including:

- https://payments.google.com/payments/apis-secure/get_legal_document?ldo=0&ldt=privacynotice
- https://policies.google.com/privacy
